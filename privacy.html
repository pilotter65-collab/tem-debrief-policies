Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-09-03 · TEM Debrief Publisher

2.1What is collected

This application collects and stores only its own operator's authentication credentials (OAuth access and refresh tokens) issued directly by YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, and Bilibili. These tokens exist solely to authorize the application to publish content to the operator's own accounts on those platforms.

No personal data is collected from any third party. This application has no public-facing sign-up, login, or data submission of any kind.

2.2Where it's stored

  • Authentication tokens are stored locally, on the operator's own device.
  • Tokens are never transmitted to any party other than the issuing platform's own API, for the sole purpose of authenticating publish requests.

2.3What is not done

  • No data is sold, rented, or shared with advertisers or other third parties.
  • No analytics or tracking scripts run on this policy page beyond what its hosting provider inherently logs.
  • No data is collected from viewers of the published video content itself.

2.4Retention & revocation

Stored tokens are retained only as long as needed to operate the application, and can be revoked at any time directly from each platform's own account security settings (for example, Google Account permissions, or TikTok's connected-apps settings) — independent of this application.
